This project implements a real-time procedural vegetation system in exploring how L-Systems can generate
botanically plausible plant structures. Environmental parameters including phototropism (effect of light on
plants), gravitropism (effect of gravity), wind, seasonal changes, and space constraints are integrated into
the project. All environmental parameters in addition to plant placement and visual settings are fully
configurable in real time. Eight different species of plant have been implemented to showcase the flexibility
of L-Systems and highlight different biological processes.
